# Module 1 - Foundation

## Objective
Reset expectations: this workshop is about operating systems in production, not just chatting with an agent.

## Key discussion points
- why incidents take too long to resolve
- why dashboards alone are not enough
- why cross-layer RCA is slow
- why context and guardrails matter

## Facilitator prompts
- What slows your team down during incidents today?
- Which tools do you switch between most often?
- What knowledge is usually trapped in past incidents?

## Expected outcome
Participants can articulate the difference between:
- monitoring
- investigation
- remediation
- institutional learning
